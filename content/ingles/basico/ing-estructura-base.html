Módulo 5: Pronombres Personales

Objetivo

Usar los pronombres personales correctamente en contexto real.

Vocabulario: Pronombres

PronombreSignificadoUso
IYoSiempre mayúscula
youTú / Usted / UstedesSingular y plural
heÉlHombres
sheEllaMujeres
itEso/ElloCosas, animales, clima
weNosotrosGrupo que incluye al hablante
theyEllos/EllasGrupo de personas o cosas

Módulo 6: Verbo BE (Uso Práctico)

Objetivo

Usar el verbo BE en afirmaciones, negaciones y preguntas de forma natural.

Vocabulario: Formas del Verbo BE

PronombreForma completaContracción
IamI'm
YouareYou're
HeisHe's
SheisShe's
ItisIt's
WeareWe're
TheyareThey're

Chunks: Afirmaciones con BE

CategoríaEjemplos Escuchar
IdentidadI'm Carlos / She's a teacher
OrigenI'm from Peru / He's from Japan
EstadoI'm tired / We're hungry
UbicaciónI'm at home / They're at work
EdadI'm 25 / She's 30 years old
DescripciónIt's big / They're nice

Chunks: Negaciones con BE

Forma completaContracción común
I am notI'm not
You are notYou're not / You aren't
He is notHe's not / He isn't
She is notShe's not / She isn't
It is notIt's not / It isn't
We are notWe're not / We aren't
They are notThey're not / They aren't

Frases comunes:

Chunks: Preguntas con BE

EstructuraEjemplo Escuchar
Am I...?Am I late?
Are you...?Are you ready?
Is he/she/it...?Is she coming?
Are we...?Are we there yet?
Are they...?Are they open?

Chunks: Respuestas Cortas

PreguntaRespuesta SíRespuesta No Escuchar
Are you ready?Yes, I amNo, I'm not
Is he your friend?Yes, he isNo, he isn't
Are they coming?Yes, they areNo, they aren't

Listening: Diálogo con BE

> A: Hi! Are you Maria?

> B: Yes, I am. And you are...?

> A: I'm Carlos. Nice to meet you!

> B: Nice to meet you too. Are you from here?

> A: No, I'm not. I'm from Colombia.
