Modales en Pasado

Objetivo

Hablar de obligaciones, habilidades y posibilidades del pasado con precisión quirúrgica.

Modal + have + participio

EstructuraSignificadoEjemplo Escuchar
must have + p.p.casi seguro que pasóThe build failed; someone must have pushed broken code
can't/couldn't have + p.p.casi seguro que NO pasóShe can't have finished — the exam is 3 hours long
may/might/could have + p.p.posible que pasaraHe might have missed your message
should have / ought to have + p.p.era lo correcto y no se hizo (crítica/arrepentimiento)We should have written tests
would have + p.p.pasa en condicional 3I would have helped if you'd asked
needn't have + p.p.se hizo pero no era necesarioYou needn't have brought food — we ordered pizza
Distingue: didn't need to do = no era necesario, así que no lo hice. needn't have done = lo hice, pero era innecesario. Diferencia de acción real, no solo de tono.

Habilidad pasada: could vs was able to

Habilidad general:  I could swim at five.
Logro puntual:      We were able to restore the backup.
                    (aquí "could" NO funciona: ✗ "We could restore")

Subjuntivo y Fórmulas Fijas

El subjuntivo mandativo

Tras verbos de exigencia/sugerencia, la tercera persona va SIN -s y el verbo BE queda en su forma base:

DisparadorEjemplo Escuchar
demand / insist / requireThe manager insists that everyone be on time
suggest / propose / recommendI suggest that he review the contract
it is essential / vital / crucial thatIt's crucial that the server stay online

Fórmulas fosilizadas (siempre subjuntivo)

  • Come what may = pase lo que pase.
  • Be that as it may = sea como sea / aun así.
  • Suffice it to say = baste decir que...
  • God forbid = Dios no lo quiera.
  • Far be it from me to... = lejos esté de mí... (disclaimer irónico)

Subjuntivo vs indicativo: matiz real

"We recommend that he IS notified."     → hecho rutinario
"We recommend that he BE notified."     → requisito formal
                                        (legal, corporativo)

En conversación ambos existen; el subjuntivo
eleva el registro al instante.

El inglés tiene MUCHO menos subjuntivo que el español. Si te suena raro, es normal: solo sobrevive en estos nichos formales.

Ellipsis y Substitution

Ellipsis: omitir lo obvio

Los nativos cortan todo lo repetible. La frase queda corta pero completa:

Frase completaNatural con ellipsis Escuchar
(Are you) Coming?Coming?
(I) Haven't seen you in ages!Haven't seen you in ages!
(It) Sounds good (to me).Sounds good.
(Do you) Need a hand?Need a hand?
She can come if she wants to comeShe can come if she wants to

Substitution con do/so/one

PalabraReemplaza aEjemplo Escuchar
do/does/didverbo completoHe works more than she does. "You fixed it!" — "I did."
sotoda una cláusula afirmativa"Will it rain?" — "I think so."
nottoda una cláusula negativa"Is it ready?" — "I'm afraid not."
one/onessustantivo contableThis laptop is faster than the old one

Respuestas nativas rápidas

"Hope so!" · "Seems like it." · "Could be."
"Wouldn't put it past him." (= es capaz)
"So I've heard." · "Says who?" · "Why not?"
"You'd better not." · "I'd rather you didn't."

Entrenar esto cambia tu ritmo: dejarás de responder con frases completas de aprendiz ("Yes, I will go to the party") y empezarás a responder como adulto nativo ("Sounds great! Count me in" ).

Discourse Markers Sofisticados

Marcadores que organizan tu discurso

Los conectores básicos (and, but, because) ya los dominas. Estos son los que usan los hablantes sofisticados para guiar la conversación:

MarcadorFunciónEjemplo Escuchar
That said,contraste tras concederThe plan is solid. That said, we need plan B
Having said that,= that saidIt's expensive. Having said that, quality pays off
By and large,en generalBy and large, users loved the redesign
All things considered,sopesando todoAll things considered, we shipped on time
To be fair,concediendo algoTo be fair, the docs were outdated too
If anything,todo lo contrario / si acasoHe wasn't rude — if anything, too polite
At any rate,en cualquier caso (cerrar tema)At any rate, let's vote and move on
Long story short,resumiendoLong story short, we missed the deadline
For what it's worth,por si sirve de algoFor what it's worth, I'd take the offer
Come to think of it,ahora que lo piensoCome to think of it, he never signed the NDA

Marcadores de tiempo para pensar

Gana segundos sin sonar torpe mientras estructuras tu idea:

  • Well..., So...: arranques neutros.
  • Let me think / Let me see: pide pausa explícita.
  • How can I put this...: busca tacto para algo delicado.
  • Off the top of my head: sin verificar datos ("Off the top of my head, around 200 users" ).

Práctica final

Conecta estas ideas usando un marcador distinto cada vez:

  1. La app funciona bien. / Hay bugs menores. (that said)
  2. No terminé la migración. / En resumen, retrasamos el release. (long story short)
  3. Mi opinión puede no valer nada. / Yo elegiría React. (for what it's worth)
