El Pitch Personal

Objetivo

Sobrevivir los primeros 90 segundos: "Tell me about yourself" decide el tono de toda la entrevista.

Estructura PRESENTE → PASADO → FUTURO (90 seg)

PRESENTE  "I'm a backend developer at Acme, where I
           work mainly with Node.js and PostgreSQL."
PASADO    "Before that, I spent three years building
           e-commerce platforms, which is where I got
           into system design."
FUTURO    "I'm looking to move into a role with more
           architectural ownership — that's what drew
           me to this position."
Escucha y repite el pitch modelo: · ·
NO recites tu CV: cuentan una historia de dirección. Y nunca digas "as I said in my CV" — la pregunta evalúa tu comunicación, no tu memoria.

Respuestas modelo rápidas

PreguntaRespuesta segura Escuchar
Why do you want to work here?"I've been following your work on X, and your focus on [valor real] matches how I like to build software."
What are your strengths?"I'd say my biggest strength is turning vague requirements into shipped features. For example..." (+1 ejemplo concreto)
What's your weakness?"I tend to over-polish before sharing. What's worked for me is timeboxing and asking for early feedback on drafts." (+acción correctiva SIEMPRE)
Why are you leaving your job?"I've grown a lot there, but I'm ready for new challenges — specifically X. It's the right time." (NUNCA hables mal del ex-jefe)

STAR: Responder por Competencias

Objetivo

Todas las preguntas "cuéntame una vez que..." se responden con STAR. Tener 4-5 historias preparadas cubre el 90% de las variantes.

La estructura STAR

PasoQué incluirEjemplo (30 seg c/u) Escuchar
Situationcontexto en 1-2 frases"Our checkout was failing under Black Friday traffic."
TaskTU responsabilidad específica"I was asked to make it survive 10x load in two weeks."
Actionlo que HICISTE tú ("I", no "we")"I profiled the bottlenecks, added caching, and split the payment flow into async jobs."
Resultnúmero + aprendizaje"We handled 12x peak with zero downtime — and I learned to always load-test third-party APIs."

Tu banco de historias (prepara 5)

  • Un logro técnico del que estés orgulloso.
  • Un conflicto/desacuerdo y cómo lo resolviste.
  • Un error que cometiste y qué aprendiste.
  • Una vez que lideraste sin tener el título.
  • Un plazo imposible y cómo lo manejaste.
Frases puente para ganar tiempo mientras piensas: "Let me think of a good example... OK so..." , "That reminds me of a time when..." . Ganar 3 segundos es profesional; rellenar con "ehhh" no.

Preguntas Técnicas Comunes

Pensando EN VOZ ALTA (lo que realmente evalúan)

Al recibir el problema:
"Let me make sure I understand — given an array of
 orders, we want the top 5 by total amount, right?"

Mientras trabajas:
"My first instinct is a hash map to get O(n). The
 trade-off is extra memory — does that work here?"

Si te atascas:
"I'd start with the brute force version and optimize
 from there. Can I code that first?"
Escucha y repite el pensamiento en voz alta: · ·

Frases de supervivencia técnica

SituaciónFrase Escuchar
No sabes la respuesta"I haven't worked with that directly. Based on what I know about X, I'd guess... — but I'd verify it."
Necesitas una pista"Could you point me in the right direction?" / "Am I on the right track?"
Te corrigieron"Good catch — thanks! So if I fix that, then..."
Pregunta de diseño"What scale are we designing for? And is latency or consistency more critical here?"
Cierre de entrevista"What does success look like in the first 90 days for this role?"
En preguntas de salario NO des un número primero: "Do you have a range budgeted for this role?" . Si insisten: da rango investigado, no cifra puntual.

Práctica final

Graba un mock interview completo (15 min): pitch + 2 STAR + 1 pregunta técnica pensando en voz alta. Escucha la grabación y cuenta tus muletillas; repite hasta reducir la mitad.
