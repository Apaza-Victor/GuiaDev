Calcos de frase que suenan fatal

"Te llamo luego"      → ✗ "I call you later"  ✓ "I'll call you later"
"Hace dos años"       → ✗ "It makes two years" ✓ "Two years ago"
"Tengo hambre"        → ✗ "I have hunger"     ✓ "I'm hungry"
"Dame un minuto"      → ✗ "Give me a minute"   ✓ correcto, este sí
"No importa"          → ✗ "It doesn't matter"  ✓ o mejor: "No worries"
"Sí, claro"           → ✓ "Sure thing!" / "Of course!"

"I have a doubt" es EL falso amigo frasesil del mundo corporativo latino. En inglés una duda = question. "Doubt" implica desconfianza: "I doubt he's honest" = dudo que sea honesto.
