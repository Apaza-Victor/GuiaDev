Verbos de Infraestructura

Objetivo

Los phrasal verbs que usan los devs a diario para hablar de entornos, deploys y releases. Aprende el verbo COMPLETO: set up NO es "set".

Crear y destruir entornos

Phrasal verbSignificadoEjemplo
set upconfigurar / montarI set up the CI pipeline.
spin uplevantar (servidores/instancias)Spin up a staging cluster.
tear downtumbar / destruir lo levantadoTear down the test env later.
boot uparrancar (máquina/OS)The container boots up in 2 s.
shut downapagar (proceso/máquina)We shut down the old instance.
roll outdesplegar de forma gradualWe rolled out the fix to 10%.
roll backrevertir un releaseWe rolled back production.
"spin up / tear down" van de la mano en tests e infra efímera: "spin up a database for the integration test, tear it down after." Es el inglés natural para tu docker-compose.

Roll out vs Roll back

  • roll out: lo lanzas gradualmente: "rolling out a canary release."
  • roll back: si algo sale mal, lo reviertes: "roll back to the previous build."
  • Ambos son separables: "roll the fix out" es válido, pero "roll out the fix" suena más nativo.
"We spun up a temporary environment, rolled out the hotfix to 10%
of users, and rolled back within an hour when the logs spiked."

Práctica

Completa con el phrasal verb correcto (set up / spin up / tear down / roll out / roll back):

  1. "Can you ______ a sandbox so I can test the webhook?"
  2. "We should ______ the old VMs after the migration."
  3. "If the metrics look bad, we'll ______ the release."

Modelo: "Can you spin up a sandbox so I can test the webhook?" / "We should tear down the old VMs after the migration." / "If the metrics look bad, we'll roll back the release."

Verbos de Colaboración

Git, código y compañeros: la rutina

Phrasal verbSignificadoEjemplo
check outclonar/ramificar y explorarCheck out the feature branch.
check inhacer commit / compartirCheck in your changes.
hook upconectar (sistema/API)Hook the webhook up to Slack.
wire upcablear/conectar componentesWire the button up to the store.
pick upretomar / encargarse deI'll pick up that ticket.
follow updar seguimiento / hacer alcanceI'll follow up on the review.
reach outcontactar / comunicarseReach out to the SRE team.
loop inmeter a alguien al hiloLoop the PM in on this.
"Pick up a ticket" es la forma nativa de "agarrar un ticket". Y "loop someone in" = centrar en la conversación; "keep me in the loop" = mantener al corriente.

Matices de equipo

  • follow up: en reunión: "I'll follow up with you after the standup." Sujeto a error de hispano: no digas "agarrar el follow-up"; di "follow up with".
  • reach out: más cálido que "contact": "Feel free to reach out if you have questions."
  • check in (no git): "I'll check in with you later" = me pondré al día contigo.

Práctica

Traduce al inglés con phrasal verbs:

  1. "Voy a revisar la rama y conectar el webhook a Slack."
  2. "¿Puedes meter al QA en el hilo y darle seguimiento al bug?"
  3. "Retomo ese ticket y le escribo al equipo de infra."

Modelo: "I'll check out the branch and hook the webhook up to Slack." / "Can you loop QA in and follow up on the bug?" / "I'll pick up that ticket and reach out to the infrastructure team."

Verbos de Resolución

Encontrar, descomponer y salir del apuro

Phrasal verbSignificadoEjemplo
look upbuscar/consultarLet me look it up in the docs.
break downdesglosar / descomponerBreak the task down into steps.
fall back onrecurrir a un plan BWe fell back on the cache.
figure outdescifrar / resolverWe figured out the root cause.
work aroundsortear / dar el rodeo, sin arreglo realWe worked around the bug.
"Figure out" es EL verbo del debugging: "I can't figure out why the test fails." Es separable: "I've got to figure this out." Y "workaround" (sustantivo, una palabra) viene del phrasal "work around".

Fall back on vs Work around

  • fall back on: tienes un plan B: "If the API is down, we fall back on the local cache."
  • work around: evitas el problema sin arreglar la causa: "We worked around the SSL issue by pinning the cert."
  • En el reporte de incidente: "We found a workaround, but the fix ships next week."
"We broke the incident down, figured out it was a race condition,
fell back on the previous flag config, and deployed a workaround."

Práctica

Traduce estas frases de debugging:

  1. "No logro descifrar por qué crashea en producción; voy a buscar en los logs."
  2. "Desglosa el epic en tareas y recurrimos al caché si se cae la API."
  3. "Vamos a sortear el problema de los headers por ahora."

Modelo: "I can't figure out why it crashes in production; I'm going to look it up in the logs." / "Break the epic down into tasks, and we fall back on the cache if the API goes down." / "We'll work around the headers problem for now."
