Conectores de Adición y Contraste

Objetivo

Los conectores son el pegamento del inglés escrito: sin ellos tu texto suena a lista de frases sueltas.

Adición

Formal / escritoNeutro / hablado
Moreover, the costs are highAlso, the costs are high
In addition to that, ...Besides, ... (más coloquial)
Furthermore, ...What's more, ...
As well as English, she speaks FrenchAnd also, ...

Contraste: la familia de BUT

ConectorTipo gramaticalEjemplo
butconjunción (une 2 frases)It's cheap but slow
howeveradverbio (va con punto/coma)It's cheap. However, it's slow
although / thoughconjunción subordinanteAlthough it's cheap, it's slow
even thoughénfasis de althoughEven though he was tired, he finished
despite / in spite of+ sustantivo o -ingDespite the rain / despite being tired
Error clásico: ❌ despite of the rain. Es despite the rain O in spite of the rain — nunca "despite of". Y ❌ although it's cheap but...: un solo contraste por frase.

Causa, Resultado y Secuencia

Causa y consecuencia

ConectorEjemplo
because + frase completaWe stayed because it was late
because of / due to + sustantivoWe stayed due to the storm
so (resultado)It was late, so we left
therefore (formal)The test failed; therefore, we blocked the merge
as / since (= porque, al inicio)Since you're here, help me out

Secuencia y ejemplificación

  • First / Firstly, then, after that, finally.
  • To begin with... , in conclusion... (ensayos)
  • For example / For instance, such as + sustantivo (languages such as Python).
  • That is / in other words: para reformular.
Because of ≠ because: because of va con SUSTANTIVO (because of the delay), because va con SUJETO+VERBO (because it was delayed).

Estructura del Párrafo

El modelo hamburger

Cada párrafo = una sola idea, en tres capas:

1. TOPIC SENTENCE   → la idea principal de la primera
2. SUPPORT          → 2-3 detalles, razones o ejemplos
                      (for example..., moreover...)
3. WRAP-UP          → cierra o enlaza al siguiente párrafo

Ejemplo aplicado (issue en GitHub)

The login page crashes on Safari. [TOPIC]
Specifically, users see a blank screen after
submitting valid credentials. For instance,
three reports appeared within an hour of our
last deploy. [SUPPORT]
Therefore, I suspect the new cookie logic is
the culprit. [WRAP-UP]

Checklist antes de enviar cualquier texto

  1. ¿Cada párrafo tiene UNA idea?
  2. ¿Hay al menos un conector distinto de "and" y "but"?
  3. ¿Las oraciones largas tienen coma después del conector inicial?
  4. ¿Terminaste respondiendo el "so what?"
