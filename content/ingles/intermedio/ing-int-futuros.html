Will: Predicciones y Decisiones

Objetivo

Usar will para lo espontáneo, promesas y opiniones sobre el futuro.

Cuándo usar will

UsoEjemplo
Decisión espontánea (en el momento)The line is huge. I'll call a taxi
Promesa / oferta / amenazaI'll send the report tonight. I'll help you
Predicción u opinión (creo que...)I think it will rain. Prices won't go down
Hechos futuros segurosShe will turn 30 next month
Forma corta: I'll, you'll, he'll... Negativa: won't (will not). En preguntas: Will you come? — Yes, I will.

Going To: Planes e Intenciones

Formación

am/is/are + going to + verbo base: I'm going to study tonight. They're going to move to Berlin.

Cuándo usar going to

UsoEjemplo
Plan decidido ANTES de hablarWe're going to launch in March (ya estaba decidido)
IntenciónThis year I'm going to learn English seriously
Predicción con EVIDENCIA visibleLook at those clouds! It's going to rain

Will vs Going To en predicciones

SituaciónOpción natural
Opinión sin pruebas ("I think...")will → I think our team will win
Evidencia presente (ves la señal)going to → Careful! You're going to drop the laptop
Plan preexistente vs decisión ahoraI'm going to travel in July · OK, I'll book the hotel

Present Continuous para Futuro

Arreglos con fecha y hora

Usa present continuous para planes ya agendados, especialmente con otras personas:

  • I'm meeting the client tomorrow at 10.
  • We're flying to Madrid on Friday.
  • What are you doing this weekend?

Los tres niveles de "plan"

NivelEstructuraEjemplo
Agendado (con otros)present continuousI'm seeing the dentist at 4
Intención personalgoing toI'm going to start the gym
Espontáneo / promesawillI'll join you later
Preguntas sociales típicas: "What are you doing tonight?" "Do you want to come?" — "Sure, I'll be there!" Pregunta con continuous, aceptación con will.

Futuros Avanzados: Perfecto y Continuo

Future Perfect: terminado antes de un punto

will have + participio: mira el futuro hacia atrás desde un momento posterior:

  • By December, I will have finished the course.
  • By the time you arrive, we will have eaten.
  • In 5 years, she will have worked here for a decade.

Future Continuous: en progreso en el futuro

will be + verbo-ing:

  • This time tomorrow, I will be flying to Lima.
  • Don't call at 8 — I'll be having dinner.
  • We will be working on v2 next quarter.

Práctica combinada

Completa con el futuro correcto (will, going to, continuous, perfect):

  1. A: The phone's ringing. B: OK, I ___ answer it. (espontáneo → will)
  2. By 2030, robots ___ replace most manual jobs. (opinión → will)
  3. Look at his calendar — he ___ give three talks next week. (agenda → is going to)
  4. By June, I ___ complete the bootcamp. (terminado antes de → will have completed)
