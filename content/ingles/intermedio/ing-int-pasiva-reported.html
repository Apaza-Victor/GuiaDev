La Voz Pasiva

Objetivo

Enfocarte en la acción o el objeto, no en quién lo hizo. Es el modo nativo de documentación técnica y noticias.

Formación

be (en el tiempo que sea) + participio (+ by agente):

TiempoActivaPasiva
PresentThey build housesHouses are built
PastShakespeare wrote HamletHamlet was written by Shakespeare
Present PerfectSomeone has stolen my bikeMy bike has been stolen
FutureThey will announce it soonIt will be announced soon
ModalYou must sign the formThe form must be signed

Pasiva en contexto técnico

"The database is updated every night."
"The feature was released last week."
"All requests are logged and encrypted."
"This bug has been fixed in v2.1."
Usa pasiva cuando el agente es obvio, desconocido o irrelevante. Si importa QUIÉN lo hizo, quédate con la activa.

Reported Speech: Statements

Contar lo que alguien dijo

Cambios clave al reportar: retrocede el tiempo verbal y ajusta pronombres/tiempo-lugar.

DirectoReportado
"I am tired"He said he was tired
"I work here"She said she worked there
"I saw him yesterday"He said he had seen him the day before
"I have finished"She said she had finished
"I will call you"He said he would call me
"I can help"She said she could help

Cambios de tiempo y lugar

DirectoReportado
nowthen / at that moment
today / tonightthat day / that night
yesterdaythe day before
tomorrowthe next day
this / thesethat / those
herethere
Si la información SIGUE siendo cierta, el backshift es opcional: "She said she lives/lived in Lima." Y ojo: said (sin persona) vs told (con persona): He said hello / He told ME hello.

Preguntas y Órdenes Reportadas

Preguntas reportadas: ¡sin estructura de pregunta!

Desaparecen do/does/did y el orden auxiliar-sujeto. Vuelven al orden normal:

DirectaReportada
"Where do you live?"He asked where I lived
"What time is it?"She asked what time it was
"Do you speak English?"They asked if/whether I spoke English
"Did you finish?"He asked if I had finished
❌ He asked where do I live. → ✅ He asked where I live(d). Las preguntas reportadas terminan en punto, no en signo de interrogación.

Órdenes, peticiones y sugerencias

Cambian de verbo + estructura infinitiva:

DirectoReportado
"Close the door!"She told me to close the door
"Don't touch that!"He told us not to touch it
"Can you help me?"She asked me to help her
"Let's take a break"He suggested taking a break
"I'll do it, honestly!"She promised to do it

Práctica

Reporta estas frases (ayer):

  1. "I'll send the PR tonight." → He said...
  2. "Are you coming to standup?" → She asked...
  3. "Don't deploy on Friday!" → They told us...
