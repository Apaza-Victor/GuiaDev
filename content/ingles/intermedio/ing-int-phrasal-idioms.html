Phrasal Verbs con GET

Objetivo

Los phrasal verbs son verbos + partícula cuyo significado cambia por completo. No se traducen: se memorizan como palabras nuevas.

Los 10 con GET que más aparecen

PhrasalSignificadoEjemplo
get uplevantarseI get up at 6:30
get along (with)llevarse bienWe get along really well
get oversuperar algoIt took weeks to get over the flu
get byarreglárselasI can get by with my basic English
get back tovolver a contactarI'll get back to you tomorrow
get togetherreunirseLet's get together next week
get rid ofdeshacerse deGet rid of that old code
get used toacostumbrarse aYou'll get used to the accent
get away withsalirse con la suyaHe never gets away with being late
get around toencontrarle tiempo a algoI finally got around to fixing it
Separable vs inseparable: "turn it off" ✓ / "get over it" ✓ pero ❌ "look after it them"... Regla rápida: con pronombre (it, him), si el verbo es separable el pronombre va EN MEDIO: turn it down, no ~~turn down it~~.

Phrasal Verbs con TAKE y PUT

Con TAKE

PhrasalSignificadoEjemplo
take offdespegar / quitarseThe plane takes off at 9. Take off your shoes
take onasumir (responsabilidad)She took on too many projects
take overtomar el controlA new CTO took over in January
take backretirar (lo dicho) / devolverI take back what I said
take upempezar un hobby / ocuparHe took up photography

Con PUT

PhrasalSignificadoEjemplo
put offposponerDon't put off your English practice
put up withtolerar / aguantarI won't put up with slow WiFi
put onponerse (ropa) / subir de pesoPut on a jacket. I've put on weight
put togetherarmar / prepararShe put together a great presentation
put downdejar en un lugar / anotarPut down your phone and listen

Práctica

Completa con el phrasal correcto:

  1. The meeting was ___ until Monday. (pospuesta → put off)
  2. How do you ___ ___ ___ his constant jokes? (tolerar → put up with)
  3. I need to ___ ___ ___ you about the offer. (contactarte → get back to)
