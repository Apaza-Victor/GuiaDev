Práctica

Completa con el phrasal correcto:

  1. The meeting was ___ until Monday. (pospuesta → put off)
  2. How do you ___ ___ ___ his constant jokes? (tolerar → put up with)
  3. I need to ___ ___ ___ you about the offer. (contactarte → get back to)
